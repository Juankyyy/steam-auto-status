<!-- title: v0.2.1 - The Identity Update -->

Same lightweight tray app that keeps your Steam presence in sync with your schedule — now wearing its own mark, with your presence visible right in the tray and a connection that recovers on its own.

## 🎨 A mark of its own

The app has a face now (the ring, the person and the state dot), and the tray icon itself tells you how you are seen on Steam.

- **New logo**: the mark sits in the panel header and in Settings > About, and the app icon — installer, window and tray — is generated from the same SVG source.
- **Presence in the tray**: the tray icon's top-right dot is green when you are Online and slate when Invisible, so you can check your presence at a glance. It only redraws when the state actually changes (no flicker).

## ✨ What's new

- **No more stuck "Connecting…"**: a watchdog restarts a logon (45 s) or an automatic reconnection (3 min) that Steam never answers. Retries back off from 10 s up to 5 min — 10 min when Steam asks to slow down — and rapid Reconnect clicks no longer chain logons.
- **Quieter sessions**: every session starts with a unique random logonID, so two running instances no longer expel each other, and the logon packet is minimal (no token or web-session requests in the middle of it). Steam's own client reprocesses its friends list on every new session, so the app now keeps those to a minimum — the README FAQ tells you what to do in the rare case the list looks empty.
- **Token renewal on its own schedule**: the saved session token now renews in the background — never during a logon, and preferably while your Steam client is closed.
- **README FAQ**: how to update over your current install (config, token and logs are kept) and what to do when the friends list looks empty after a reconnect.

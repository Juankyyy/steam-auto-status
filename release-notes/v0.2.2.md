<!-- title: v0.2.2 - The Bilingual Update -->

The app speaks two languages now: switch anytime and the panel, the tray and your notifications all follow. Also in this release: a tucked-away developer mode and a rebuilt README.

## 🌍 Español & English

Spanish and English are equally at home: pick one in Settings > Language and the whole app changes instantly, no restart needed.

- **One setting, everywhere**: the panel, the tray menu, the tooltip and Windows notifications all read `config.json`, so a single switch keeps everything in sync.
- **First run**: with no choice saved, the app starts in your Windows language (a Spanish Windows starts in Spanish, everything else in English) and remembers your pick.
- **Times read naturally**: schedules and countdowns use 24 h in Spanish and 12 h AM/PM in English, while the schedule editor keeps following Windows.
- **English logs, always**: the technical log and the copied diagnostics stay in English on purpose, so a bug report reads the same whatever interface language you use.

## ✨ What's new

- **Developer mode**: the test tools (force Online/Invisible for a while, live log) moved out of the dashboard into their own tab, hidden by default. Turn it on in Settings > Application when you need it; the tab appears and disappears with the switch.
- **New README**: the repository page opens with the lockup, adapted to light and dark themes, shows a screenshot of the panel and reorganizes the feature list and FAQ.

🎮 **Steam Auto Status v0.1.0** — first release!

A lightweight Windows tray app that keeps your Steam presence in sync with your schedule, with no manual toggling. 🕒

## ✨ What's included

- 🕒 **Automatic schedule** — you show up as **Online** during your configured window (default: Mon–Fri, 17:30 → 01:00, local time) and **Invisible** outside of it. Works even with the Steam client closed.
- 👻 **Always-on session** — the app keeps a parallel "ghost client" session and switches your presence for you. It never plays games, so it won't interfere with your sessions or VAC.
- 📱 **Easy login** — scan a QR code with the Steam mobile app (or use username/password with mobile confirmation). The token is stored locally, and future launches require nothing.
- 🖥️ **Tray menu** — open the panel, "Online for 30 min", "Invisible now", pause/resume automation, start with Windows, and quit.
- ⚙️ **Settings panel** — schedule & weekdays, pause, notifications, temporary overrides, data folder, and live status.
- 🪶 **Built to run 24/7** — lightweight shell (Rust + WebView2) with a Node sidecar; low resource usage.

## 📥 Installation

1. Download **[steam-auto-status_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe](https://github.com/Juankyyy/steam-auto-status/releases/download/v0.1.0/steam-auto-status_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe)** and run it — per-user install, no admin rights, available in English & Spanish.
2. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher (the app isn't code-signed): **More info → Run anyway**.
3. It lives in the system tray (next to the clock): click the icon to open the panel and sign in with the QR code.

## 🔐 Your data

- Config, token, and logs live in `Documents\steam-auto-status\` (not AppData) and **survive reinstalls and uninstalls**.

## ✅ Requirements

- Windows 10/11 (x64). WebView2 ships with Windows 10/11; the installer only downloads it if missing.
